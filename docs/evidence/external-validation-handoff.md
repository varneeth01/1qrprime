## Native configuration handoff

- Android application ID: `in.oneqr.prime`.
- iOS bundle identifier: `in.oneqr.prime`.
- Expo version: `1.0.0`; EAS production profile uses remote versioning and an Android app bundle.
- Android target/compile SDK: 36; minimum SDK: 26; required NDK: `27.0.12077973`.
- Android notification permission: `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`; camera and media permissions are explicitly blocked because QR sharing uses generated assets rather than camera capture.
- iOS notification behavior is provided by `expo-notifications`; privacy manifest and non-exempt encryption declarations are present.
- Native deep link scheme: `oneqrprime://`. Universal Links/App Links are intentionally absent so public QR scans open the web Customer Action Page without requiring the app.
- Icons and splash assets are under `apps/mobile/assets`; store screenshots must be captured from signed builds, not local QA screenshots.

## Deep-link and QR behavior

The native app deep link `oneqrprime://` is available only for explicitly supported app flows. Customer QR scans currently resolve through the public HTTPS Customer Action Page, and the separate payment QR resolves to a UPI payload. Universal Links and Android App Links are not enabled. A regular camera scan must not require the merchant app, and the product must not claim automatic QR-to-native-app opening or scanner-specific routing without physical-device evidence.

The server QR endpoint constructs page payloads from `PUBLIC_ORIGIN`; payment payloads are generated separately by the `basic_upi` adapter. The local QR fixture may use `http://localhost:5173` for development only. A staging/operator run must use `scripts/staging-origin-check.mjs` and a real HTTPS `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` before producing printable staging assets.

## Evidence rules

Do not record a configuration parse as service connectivity. Do not record a JavaScript export as an APK/AAB or iOS archive. Do not record a redirect, screenshot, or UPI-app return as payment confirmation. Keep the separate Customer Action Page QR and UPI QR fallback until physical-device evidence proves otherwise.
