# App Store metadata draft

## Name

1QR Prime

## Subtitle

Manage your business QR

## Description

1QR Prime helps local merchants manage a digital business page, printed QR assets, menus, customer orders, payment destinations, and team access.

Customers can view your page without installing an app. Merchants can update business details, catalogue availability, order status, payment-route state, QR artwork, and notifications from the companion app.

The app does not request UPI PINs, bank passwords, or customer payment credentials. Customer payments for physical goods and services are handled through the configured merchant destination and remain separate from merchant-plan entitlements.
