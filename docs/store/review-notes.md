# Store review notes template

1QR Prime is a native merchant management app, not a website wrapper. Reviewers can sign in with the supplied demo merchant account, edit the business page, toggle menu availability, open the order desk, inspect payment-route states, and export/delete the account.

Customers use the public page without installing 1QR Prime. The app does not sell merchant subscriptions in the submitted build; plan state is displayed from the merchant service. Customer payments are for physical food/goods/services and are separate from app entitlements.

The submitted build does not claim automatic QR-to-native-app opening. Printed page QR scans open the HTTPS Customer Action Page; the separate UPI QR is used for payment-app flows. Universal Links and Android App Links are not enabled.

Provide a live HTTPS API, demo credentials, sample QR, test merchant page, support contact, and exact steps before submission. Never use a production merchant VPA or customer banking credentials in review materials.
