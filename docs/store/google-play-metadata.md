# Google Play metadata draft

## Short description

Manage your business page, QR, orders, menu and payment destinations.

## Full description

1QR Prime is the merchant app for your 1QR business presence. Update your public page, manage catalogue availability, receive order alerts, handle restaurant orders, prepare printable QR artwork, and manage verified payment destinations.

Customers do not need this app or an account to open a merchant’s public page.

1QR Prime never asks for UPI PINs or bank passwords. Payment confirmation is only shown when a configured provider can verify it; a basic UPI handoff remains explicitly pending until the merchant or provider confirms it.

## Listing inputs

- Category: Business
- Target audience: business owners and authorised staff
- Content rating: complete the current Play questionnaire; no child-directed experience is intended.
- Privacy policy: set operated HTTPS URL.
- Support email/URL: set operated support identity.
- Screenshots: capture signed build on supported phone and tablet dimensions after internal testing.

## Data safety draft

Collected: account email, merchant profile, catalogue/order data, operational audit data, notification token, and customer-submitted order/request information. Purposes: app functionality, account management, order operations, security, and notifications. Data is encrypted in transit; account deletion and export are supported.
