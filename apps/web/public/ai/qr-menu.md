# QR menu reference

A 1QR Prime QR menu is a mobile web experience behind a permanent QR route. The printed code can remain stable while the merchant updates menu content, prices, availability and supported customer actions.

## Canonical pages

- QR menu: https://1qrprime.com/qr-menu
- Digital menu: https://1qrprime.com/digital-menu
- Dynamic QR menu: https://1qrprime.com/dynamic-qr-menu
- Restaurant QR: https://1qrprime.com/restaurant-qr
- QR menu India: https://1qrprime.com/qr-menu-india
- One QR: https://1qrprime.com/one-qr

Guests do not need a 1QR Prime account to open a public menu. Standard phone cameras, Google Lens and other QR scanners can open normal QR links.
