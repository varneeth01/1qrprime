# Payments, security and safety reference

- Public QR pages and private merchant controls are separate.
- A payment deep link or payment-app launch is not proof of payment.
- Merchants should verify receipt through their bank or payment provider.
- Payment destination changes are sensitive actions and should be controlled and auditable.
- Guests should verify the destination domain before entering information or paying.
- 1QR Prime is an independent product and is not affiliated with Google.
- Google Lens is one possible QR scanner; Google does not provide the 1QR Prime service.
- Never ask a guest to provide a UPI PIN or banking password to 1QR Prime.
