# 1QR Prime product reference

1QR Prime connects a permanent public QR with a mobile customer page and an authenticated merchant workspace.

## Supported product areas

- Permanent business and table QR routes
- Digital menus, categories, prices and availability
- Restaurant order states and table context
- Customer-facing actions
- UPI/payment destination handoff with explicit confirmation boundaries
- Staff accounts and permissions
- Analytics based on recorded product activity
- Mobile-friendly merchant workflows

## Product boundaries

1QR Prime should not be described as a full POS, accounting, inventory or banking system unless those capabilities are explicitly implemented. Opening a payment application is a handoff and is not confirmation that money was received.
